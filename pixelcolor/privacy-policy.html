PixelColor Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

PixelColor is a color-by-number app on Google Play and the Apple App Store. This one policy covers both the Android app and the iOS app. It explains what the app handles on your device and what advertising and store partners may receive. We do not run user accounts, and we do not operate a server that stores your pictures or coloring progress.

Contact: devmlache@gmail.com
Web: https://github.com/devmlache-lang

Information stored on your device

Coloring progress, settings, coin balance, tool counts, and whether you have removed forced ads are saved in local storage on the phone or tablet. This information stays on that device. Uninstalling the app removes it.

If you choose a photo, the app reads that image from your library and builds a coloring board on the device. We do not upload the photo to a server we operate, and we do not use it to show ads.

When you tap save or share on a finished picture, the image is written to a file you choose or handed to the system share sheet. We receive no copy of that share.

Advertising

On both Android and iOS, the app shows ads through Google AdMob. A full-screen ad can appear when you open a picture to color and again when you leave coloring. In the shop, you can choose to watch a rewarded ad to receive coins. Buying ad removal turns off those forced full-screen ads on that device. The rewarded ad remains optional.

Google may process device and ad data under its own policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. That can include an IP address, ad interactions, and the advertising identifier provided by the operating system (Android Advertising ID, or Apple’s Identifier for Advertisers when you allow tracking). You can limit ad personalization in your device settings.

Purchases

Coins and ad removal are sold by Google Play on Android and by the App Store on iOS. Google or Apple processes the payment. We do not receive your card number. We receive the product result needed to grant coins or turn off forced ads on that device. Restoring a purchase uses the store account on that platform.

What we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email, or contacts inside the app.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • The app does not include a separate analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

Permissions

  • Photos: on Android and iOS, used only when you pick a picture to turn into a coloring page.
  • Network: used to load ads and to complete store purchases.

Children

PixelColor is a general-audience app. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change.
